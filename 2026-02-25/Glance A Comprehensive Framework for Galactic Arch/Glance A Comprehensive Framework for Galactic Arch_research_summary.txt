3. **Integration of Techniques**: It integrates multiple state-of-the-art techniques such as stellar population synthesis (Fado and Starlight), kinematic extraction (pPXF and Bayes-LOSVD), and dynamical modelling (Dynamite) into a streamlined workflow.

# 7. Technical Keywords
1. **Integral-field spectroscopy** - A technique that provides spatially resolved spectroscopic information over an entire region.
2. **Galactic extinction correction** - Adjusting for dust obscuration to get accurate light measurements from stars.
3. **Dynamical modelling** - Simulating the motion and behavior of stars and gas within a galaxy to understand its structure and history.
4. **Exponential model** - A simple mathematical representation often used to describe the surface brightness profile of galactic disks.
